# My English Adventure - 2nd Grade

Interactive English exam for 2nd grade, published with GitHub Pages.

## V8 highlights
- Adventure-map experience inspired by educational game/quiz patterns.
- 5 missions with 4 questions each.
- One question at a time.
- First-try stars, XP, mission badges and confetti.
- Immediate feedback and retry after mistakes.
- Best score saved locally on the device.
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- 20 question illustrations.
- Female neural English voice (`en-US-JennyNeural`) rendered as real MP3 files.
- `Listen` and slower `Slow` playback buttons.
- No browser SpeechSynthesis dependency for question audio.

## Topics
1. My House
2. Prepositions of Place: in, on, under
3. Where's...? / Where are...? — It's / They're
4. Clothes, Colors, His & Her
5. Review 9
